About

LGDA Technologies is a biometric wellness system built by founder Tahmire Robinson. The music — recorded under the name Slator Blacc — is designed to be measured, not just heard: every track is played in live biometric sessions where a phone camera measures heart rate and heart-rate variability (RMSSD) while the music plays, and the session data is published alongside each track.

LGDA Technologies owns 100% of the masters and 100% of the publishing for the catalog below — no label, no publisher. One-stop clearance: one agreement clears both sides. Contact: lgda.technologies31@gmail.com

Wellness music. Not a medical device; HRV values are estimates for research and educational use only.
