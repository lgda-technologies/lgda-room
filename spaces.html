Put the LGDA Room in your space.

The room reads the person sitting in front of it, and the music and light answer back. Any space with a screen and a camera can run it. Nothing to install, nothing to buy, thirty seconds to set up.

What you need

  • Any phone, tablet, computer, or TV with a camera
  • Light on the visitor’s face (the camera reads blood flow; it needs light)
  • That’s it. No hardware, no account, no install, no cost.

Set it up

1
Open lgda-technologies.github.io/lgda-room on the device.
2
Go fullscreen, so the page fills the screen. The screen is the room’s light.
3
Leave it there. Visitors tap Start the loop, sit still, and run a free five-minute session. The device stays on the page between visitors.

What visitors get

  • A free session: one minute of quiet baseline, then a full track while the camera measures heart rate and HRV
  • Their own numbers, before and after, side by side
  • The room light shifting with their state in real time
  • A log they can copy for themselves. Nothing leaves the device unless the visitor chooses to share it.

Make it work well

  • Face the visitor toward the light, not away from it
  • Device at eye level, about an arm’s length away
  • Screen brightness up — the glow is part of the measurement
  • A quiet-ish spot helps, but the room works anywhere a person can sit still for a few minutes
